Applied Invention

Engineer / Researcher  ·  Summer 2019 – Summer 2025
Burbank, California

I worked on a variety of projects as part of the Mechanical R&D team. Most notably, as Engineer, I was responsible for the completion of the integrated testing and assembly, as well as installation and early commissioning phases, of the timekeeping and calendar mechanisms for the Clock of the Long Now. Project work involved scrupulous engineering, fabrication, and testing for hundreds of bespoke CNC parts. Other projects included ARM microcontroller programming and data collection for an experimental Smart Tire IMU insert, as well as production part design for an autonomous CNC greenhouse overbot.

Rhode Island School of Design

Digital Fabrication Technician  ·  Fall 2015 – Fall 2018
Providence, Rhode Island

I launched a new lab at RISD, establishing programming and training routines on CNC and workshop equipment for a department of 500+ students and faculty.
