Overview

A ground-up custom CNC router build. The full assembly was designed in Autodesk Inventor with a V-slot 2040 aluminum extrusion frame, Hiwin HGR15 linear rails on all three axes, and TR8x8-4 trapezoidal anti-backlash lead screws. The machine includes a custom vacuum spoil-board system and a touch-probe tool-length sensor.
