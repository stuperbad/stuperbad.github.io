Overview

A project built around the 2023 Hackaday Superconference badge — an annual electronics conference in Pasadena where attendees hack the conference badge into something new over the course of the weekend.

This project explored generative visual output on the badge display, including circular orbital simulations and animated planetary systems — using the badge hardware as a constrained creative canvas.
