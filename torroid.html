Overview

A toroidal form generated through parametric modeling in Rhino with Grasshopper. The surface is articulated through radial ribs whose geometry is driven by the surface curvature at each point — ribs that are shallow at the equator deepen toward the inner curve of the torus, making the underlying mathematical logic of the form legible as physical structure.

The ribs were modeled as a Grasshopper definition, resolved into planar cuts, and fabricated via CNC routing in wood. Assembly without fasteners — interlocking profiles hold the form from the inside.
