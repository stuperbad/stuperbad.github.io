Overview

A biomorphic sculpture exploring organic growth through computational modeling. The form — a broad flared cap supported on a tapered stalk — was developed as a 3D mesh in Rhino, then resolved into fabricable geometry and realized in physical material.

"Axiradial" describes the underlying organizing logic: elements emanate radially from a central vertical axis, producing the characteristic mushroom-like symmetry while allowing local curvature variation across the cap surface. The result sits between natural form and mathematical object.
