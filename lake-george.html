Lake George Triptych

Generative Landscape Studies

Type

Digital / Interactive

Year

Tools

Processing
Custom software

Category

Digital / Interactive

Overview

A three-panel digital work rooted in the landscape of Lake George, New York — a place of significant personal history. The triptych translates the visual qualities of the lake — the particular quality of light on water, the layering of shoreline and sky, the way weather changes the scene — into generative computational form.

Each panel responds to its source differently, so the three together form a conversation about perception and place: the same subject seen through three different interpretive lenses.
