Overview

An early series of furniture pieces exploring CNC-sliced construction: three-dimensional forms built from stacked and interlocking profiles cut from flat sheet material. Each piece is modeled as a solid in 3D, then sliced into its component planar cuts and nested for efficient CNC routing.

The series was an investigation into how computational toolpaths change the logic of furniture design — what becomes possible when you're not constrained by conventional joinery, and what new constraints the machine introduces in its place.
