How to play

    
    
      
  1. Draw. You get a place name. Sketch its outline from memory. You have 30 seconds, and the clock starts when you first touch the sheet. Use as many strokes as you like.
    2. 
      
  2. Fit. The real border appears under your drawing. You have 20 seconds to line it up. Drag inside your shape to move it, drag on its edge to resize it, and drag outside the shape to turn it. Resizing keeps the proportions wherever you grab the edge. To change width and height separately, press Stretch, or hold Shift while you drag. Stretching out of proportion costs up to 15 points, and a small magnet snaps you back to proportional so it never happens by accident. Scroll to zoom and right-drag to pan, or pinch with two fingers on a phone.
    3. 
      
  3. Score. Each map is worth 100, half from the outline and half from the shape. The outline half rewards a line that stays close to the real border and covers all of it. The shape half compares the filled space of your drawing with the real shape. Tap Show the math after a map to see exactly what counted, and hover or tap a map on the results screen to see its numbers again. If a score feels wrong, tell us with the buttons under it.
    4. 
    

    
Five maps a day, the same five for everyone, and they change at midnight. The No. button at the top opens the archive, where you can play any day you missed. After that, Practice gives you as many random maps as you want. The button beside it changes the look between neon, day and night. Country shapes use land borders, and the United States is the lower 48.
